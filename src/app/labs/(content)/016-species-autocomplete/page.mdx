One of my side projects involved building a registration system that linked
plants people owned with their species. The records were stored in separate
databases and linked using IDs and GraphQL. My challenge was to make a plant
registration form with autocomplete for the species. I could use Algolia and
React InstantSearch to query the species index and display results, but I was
stuck trying to link the selection to my Formik form.

After experimenting some more, I tried React Select. The trick was to use its
async select and populate the options with a callback to the Algolia index.
The callback shaped each search hit into a label and value. I could also pass
an image through to a custom option. Selecting an option set Formik's
`speciesId` field to the hit's ID.
